Sunny Silvia

Cut from the same Minya stone as Sunny Menia, but along the vein. The result is a calm beige with fine, regular parallel lines, selected block by block.

Good to know

Ask to see a vein-cut and a cross-cut piece from the same block before you choose; the two are not mixed on one surface. Selected block by block, so quantities are confirmed before order. Interior use recommended.
