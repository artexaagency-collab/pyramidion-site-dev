Basalt · Hauran, Syria

Basalt

A dense, charcoal-grey volcanic stone from the Hauran region of Syria, used there for building since Roman times. Hard, absorbs very little water and gives good grip once textured.

Good to know

Semi-slabs have a natural cut and are supplied to processors who finish them. For stock tiles and cut-to-size pieces: bush-hammered, flamed, honed, sawn, tumbled, split face and rock face. Fix strips with a C2 adhesive; mechanical support above 3 m.
