Galala

08

A cream-beige limestone with a smooth, closed surface, sold in the trade as Galala Marble. Rated for interior and exterior use.

Thickness

Good to know

One of the few Egyptian limestones that holds a true polish. Bush-hammered and sandblasted finishes are used for exterior paving and steps; polished Galala is specified for interiors only. Slabs are held in the yard at Koropi.
