Sunny Menia

06

A fine-grained Egyptian limestone in warm beige with golden undertones and a very even surface. Suited to large floors where uniformity matters.

Supply
Finishes
Polished, honed, brushed, bush-hammered, acid-washed, sandblasted

Thickness

Good to know

The most consistent stone in the range from lot to lot. Takes a polish well; honed is the usual choice for floors over 100 m². Three tones are quarried at Minya, from light gold to dark yellow; the catalogue stone is the medium tone.
