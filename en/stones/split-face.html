Split face

Stone split along its natural grain by a hydraulic splitter. The face is rugged, the edges are straight, and the pieces stack neatly in courses. Most stones in the range can be split, including Sunny Menia, Sinai Pearl and basalt.

Good to know

Split face is a natural break, not a sawn finish: relief depth varies by piece and is part of the material. Order 5 % extra for cutting around openings. Light it from the side to bring out the texture.

Technical data
Split face
Split by machine. Flatter and more regular, with straight edges, laid in courses
Rock face
Dressed by hand. Deeper relief and irregular edges, laid at random
