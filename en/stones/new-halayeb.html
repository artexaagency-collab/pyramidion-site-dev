Granite · Red Sea, Egypt

New Halayeb

A hard, medium-grained Egyptian granite, grey and white with scattered black flecks. Rated for countertops, high-traffic floors and façades.

Supply
Finishes

Good to know

Flamed is the standard finish for exterior paving and gives a slip-resistant surface. Polished slabs for countertops are sealed before installation. The pattern is bold at close range; view a full slab before specifying it for a large floor.
