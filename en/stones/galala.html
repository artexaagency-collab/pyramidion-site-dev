Galala

Named after Galala Mountain in the Suez region: a cream to off-white stone with a calm, even look, and one of Egypt's most widely exported stones. Geologically a limestone.

Good to know

One of the few Egyptian limestones that holds a true polish. Bush-hammered and sandblasted finishes are used for exterior paving and steps; polished Galala is specified for interiors only. Selections vary by quarry.
