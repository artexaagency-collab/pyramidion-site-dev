Stone is chosen before it is cut.

Pyramidion selects natural stone at the quarry and supplies it to architects, designers, contractors and importers in Greece and across Europe. Each lot is inspected before shipping and delivered with its technical data. Blocks, slabs and tiles leave our yard in Koropi, Attica; custom sizes and finishes are cut to drawing.

How we work

Three commitments

At the quarry
Blocks are selected on site for structure, colour and grain. What is rejected there never reaches the yard.
With the data
Every stone is described by its geology, not its trade name, and quoted to European standards. A lot-specific test report is issued with each order.
To the site
Packed to ISPM 15, photographed before departure, delivered by sea or road to the address on the drawing.
